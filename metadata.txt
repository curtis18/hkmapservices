It also converts HK1980 Grid and WGS84 coordinates, converts a File Geodatabase or GeoPackage, downloads files named by a URL column, unzips downloaded archives in place, exports the current map to SVG, shows both coordinate systems under the map cursor, searches for places, and compares layers or groups with Swipe and Split.
